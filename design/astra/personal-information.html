Collection policy / September 13, 2026

Protect people. Keep the record checkable.

Appearing in a record implies nothing about anyone, including wrongdoing, exposure or illness.

What we remove or hide

Private individuals’ names, personal contact details, signatures, household identifiers, financial identifiers and personal or medical details are hidden in images, OCR, search, snippets, exports and saved citations. Residents, complainants, patients, claimants and workers in a private capacity have no entity pages, search entries or suggestions.

Buildings and dates remain usable without named households. Professional actions may be indexed only with the role and supporting record: author, recipient, cc, signatory, approver, analyst, inspector or certifier. We check companion pages and older captures so a detail hidden in one record is not exposed through another.

Report a missed redaction

Use “Report personal information” on any document page. Give the Bates page and the location of the information; do not repeat the private details. The preview prepares a report locally for copying and does not send it.

Open page report →

Response standard for the live service

Acknowledge within 24 hours. Restrict the reported region while reviewing, within 24 hours of receipt; review the page and linked copies within 72 hours. If review takes longer, maintain the restriction and update the reporter every 72 hours. These are proposed service commitments, not an active reporting service in this preview.

Identity questions are refused

I can’t help identify a redacted person or reconstruct private identities. You can search building conditions, test results, dates, offices and officials’ actions on records.

When the City changes a page

Keep the citation and capture history resolvable. Apply privacy restrictions to mirrored pages, older captures, crops, OCR and exports. A restricted page resolves to a notice and safe metadata; permanence is not a way around a redaction.

City 9/11 records only

This service covers the City’s 9/11 records. Suggestions connect records through subjects, places, substances, offices and dates. No social graph or private-person discovery.
