# 911records.nyc logo marks

**Direction.** One continuous, even-weight line draws lower Manhattan in 2001, seen from the Hudson, like an architect's elevation. Heights are to scale (1 unit = 10 ft). Widths are slightly generous so the line stays legible when small.

**Buildings.** Left to right:
- Woolworth: spire.
- 3 World Financial Center: pyramid.
- 1 WTC: antenna.
- 2 WTC: no mast.
- 2 World Financial Center: dome.
- Chase Manhattan Plaza: slab.
- 40 Wall: pyramid and spire.
- 70 Pine: pointed crown.
- One New York Plaza: flat, at the Battery.

Every top reads in silhouette, and nothing post-2001 appears. The compact mark keeps only the towers and 40 Wall.

**Respect.** There is no event imagery: no plane, smoke, light, flag or gap. The towers simply stand among their neighbors at true scale.

**Revision 2 (Henry: "looks like it's on a hill").**
- The line stands on one flat baseline. Buildings rise sheer, and the ground runs a short way past the end walls.
- The staircases are gone, and the low blocks are uneven.
- The towers sit about a third of the way in. One New York Plaza at the Battery end balances the outline, so it has no central peak.
- The primary mark is wider, about 3.6:1.
