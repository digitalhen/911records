# 911records.nyc logo marks

**Direction.** One continuous stroke of the lower Manhattan skyline as it stood in 2001, seen from the Hudson. It is drawn like an architect's elevation: square corners, one weight and round caps. Heights follow the real buildings at 1 unit = 10 ft. Widths are slightly generous so the lines stay legible when small.

**Buildings.** Left to right:
- Woolworth: crown and spire.
- 3 World Financial Center: pyramid.
- 1 WTC: antenna to 1,728 ft.
- 2 WTC: no mast.
- 2 World Financial Center: dome.
- One Chase Manhattan Plaza: flat slab.
- 40 Wall Street: pyramid and spire.
- 70 Pine: pointed crown.

Each has a top that reads in silhouette. As in 2001, nothing else comes near the towers' height. No post-2001 building appears. The compact mark keeps only 3 WFC, both towers and 40 Wall.

**Respect.** There is no event imagery: no plane, smoke, light beams, flag or gap. The towers simply stand among their neighbors at true scale. The line is even and unornamented, so it reads as a records office's mark, not a memorial souvenir.

**Files.**
- `mark.svg`: primary.
- `mark-compact.svg`: 16–32 px.
- `lockup.svg`: stacked.
- `lockup-horizontal.svg`: header.
- `alt-fine.svg`: hairline, fuller skyline.
- `alt-horizon.svg`: runs into a water line.
- `alt-bold.svg`: heavy, essentials only.
- `preview.html`: all variants.

**Recommendation.** Use `mark.svg` and `lockup.svg` as the identity. Use `mark-compact.svg` and `lockup-horizontal.svg` for the favicon and header. `alt-horizon.svg` suits a wide banner or footer.
