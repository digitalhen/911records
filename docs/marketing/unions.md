# FDNY / NYPD union and 9/11 responder-org contact list

Research for an outreach email introducing 911records.nyc (free explorer over the City's ~172,000
pages of released 9/11 DEP/FDNY/DDC/DOB/DCAS/Mayor's Office records) to the New York City
firefighter and police unions, their 9/11 health/benefits arms, and allied responder organizations.
FealGood Foundation and Citizens for the Extension of the James Zadroga Act are excluded — already
contacted.

Every email below was either printed on the organization's own site (**seen on site**) or found in
a reliable third-party source, labelled as such. Nothing was guessed or pattern-constructed. Where
no email exists on the public web, the entry says **contact form only** or **phone only** and gives
the URL/number to use instead. Several official union sites (nypd-lba.org, cobanyc.org, dc37.net,
nypdcea.org, twulocal100.org's leadership pages) block automated fetches with a 403/406, so those
entries lean on phone numbers and press contacts confirmed elsewhere on the same domain.

Web research only — no emails were sent, no code was touched.

## Suggested email

**Subject:** A free way to search NYC's 172,000 pages of released 9/11 records, by address and date

Hi [Name],

My name is [Founder], and my father-in-law, Hughie Yee, died of a WTC-related cancer after a hard
fight to get VCF-certified. That fight is why I built 911records.nyc: a free, independent explorer
over the roughly 172,000 pages of 9/11 records the City released last month from DEP, FDNY, DDC,
DOB, DCAS and the Mayor's Office. The City's own portal is a single search box with no way to browse;
911records.nyc adds a 3D map of lower Manhattan, browsing by the City's own record boxes, plain-
language questions answered with page citations, and case folders and exhibit lists built for VCF
claims.

The part I think would matter most to your members: the site can be searched by address and by
date, so someone can look up the specific block they worked and the specific weeks they were there,
and pull up the sampling and monitoring pages that mention that place and time — useful evidence
for a WTC Health Program or VCF claim, and just useful to know what was actually measured where you
worked.

I'd like to offer a short briefing for your benefits staff and delegates — 20 minutes, in person or
by video, showing how the search works and how to build a case folder from it. No cost, no catch.

Would that be useful to set up?

Thank you for everything you do for the members you represent.

[Founder]
911records.nyc
