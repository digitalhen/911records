## How to send

- **Send individually, not BCC.** Every entry above has a distinct opening line built from something
  specific on that firm/org's own site — a bulk send loses that and reads as spam (and several of
  these firms are quite good at spotting a form email). A personal mail client, one at a time, with
  the subject line naming the firm, works best.
- **Lead with the personalized opening line** from the table, then the adapted body below.
- **Demo video:** `~/Downloads/911records-demo.mp4` (~4.2 MB) is small enough to attach directly to
  most emails (well under the common 25 MB limit), or offer it as a follow-up so the first email
  stays short. Either works — the body below ends with an explicit offer to demo live, which reads
  well whether or not the video is attached.
- **Where only a contact form exists**, use that form for the first outreach (don't try to guess an
  address) — several of these firms' forms route straight to the named partner's assistant. Note
  the phone number as a backup for firms where the form looks like generic intake.
- **Stagger sends** over a few days rather than all at once — easier to track replies and adjust the
  pitch if a particular angle (VCF vs. advocacy vs. litigation-record) is landing better.
