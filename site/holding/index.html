9/11 Records
911records.nyc · in preparation

The City’s 9/11 records, made searchable.

On September 8, 2026, New York City released tens of thousands of documents about its response to September 11, 2001 — air and dust testing, building-by-building sampling, inspections and internal memos. This independent project is building a way to search them, read them page by page, and cite them precisely.

What it will do

  • Search the full text of every page, or ask a question and get an answer that cites the exact pages it came from.
  • Find the records for a building: what was tested there, when, and what the City said about it.
  • Keep track of what the City adds, removes or re-redacts in later releases.

Until then

The documents are available now on the City’s official portal: sept11documents.cityofnewyork.us. The page you cite should always be the City’s own.

Personal information

The City redacted personal information before release, and says some may have been missed. This project will not surface the names or personal details of private individuals, and will act quickly on reports. If you find personal information in a City document, you can also report it directly to the City through its portal.
