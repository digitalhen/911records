Privacy

Last updated September 14, 2026

What this site collects

This site has no accounts, forms or sign-ups, and it does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information.

Like most websites, the servers that deliver these pages keep standard technical logs (IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time) to keep the site running and secure. The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes that connection information as part of delivering and protecting the site.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense to help cover its costs. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them and measure their performance. Where you have allowed it, those ads may be based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • How Google uses information from sites that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Turn off personalized advertising: Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time from the privacy link Google adds to the page.

Ads are never targeted using the content of the City’s records, and this site does not share information about what you read with advertisers.

Personal information in the records

The documents are published by the City of New York. The City redacted personal information before release and says some may have been missed. If you find personal information in a City document, you can report it to the City through its official portal. This project will not surface the names or personal details of private individuals.
